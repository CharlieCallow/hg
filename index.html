Compliance-first framework

Regulated institutions need a counterparty they can put on their risk register.

01

Institutional clients only

No retail. Onboarding restricted to regulated fund managers, market makers, CASPs, and interdealer brokers.

02

Three lines of defence

First line: business. Second line: Compliance & Risk. Third line: Internal Audit, reporting independently to the non-executive board.

03

Continuous monitoring

PEP / Adverse Media / Sanction screening on onboarding and in life. Transaction monitoring on every hop, with four-eye alert review.

04

Safeguarded funds

Client funds held in named Safeguarding Accounts at the Banking Partner, segregated via a Stichting under Wft art. 3:29a.

Regulatory footprint

Supervised in the Netherlands. Banked in Europe.

Hillgate operates under DNB supervision as an EMI and is seeking MiCAR authorisation from the AFM. Client funds are safeguarded in named accounts at the Banking Partner via a dedicated Stichting.
